# Spec

**Project name:** Lowkey
**One-line pitch:** It looks like you're doom-scrolling, but you're actually studying. Paste your notes, get a reels feed that's secretly a review.
**Who it's for + their problem:** High school and university students who scroll instead of studying.
**Theme / track:** [fill in] + ElevenLabs track
**How it fits the theme (one sentence):** [fill in]
**The wow moment:** Paste messy chem notes and a reels feed appears that's secretly a full review.

## Must have
- Paste notes, AI turns them into flashcards
- Full-screen, snap-scrolling vertical feed that looks like reels (sidebar icons, big captions)
- Tap a card to reveal the answer

## Nice to have
- Real YouTube Shorts on the topic mixed into the feed
- ElevenLabs narrator voice on each card, with captions
- "Got it / review again" tracker

## Not doing
- Instagram or TikTok videos, accounts, saving decks, a mobile app

## Example inputs
1. Grade 11 chemistry: equilibrium and Le Chatelier's principle
2. Grade 10 history: causes of World War I
3. French: passé composé vs imparfait
